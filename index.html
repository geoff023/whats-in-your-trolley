An Australian grocery story

What's in Your Trolley?

Every week, Australians push a trolley past thousands of products. Where do they come from, where do we buy them, and what is really inside?

1. Where our food comes from

Australia grows plenty of food, but our shelves still depend on the world. In 2025 we imported food from more than 170 countries. Darker countries sent us more.

Each type of food has its own trade routes. Pick a food type to see its ten biggest sources. Thicker lines carry more food.

2. Where we shop

Two chains, Woolworths and Coles, run about a third of all mapped supermarkets. Away from the coast, the nearest store can be a long drive away.
